Tooltips

Rest the pointer (or tab with the keyboard) on a button: the first tooltip waits 400 ms, the next one along the toolbar shows at once. Move onto the tooltip and it stays; Esc hides it.

Menu button

Click, Enter, Space or ↓ opens on the first item, ↑ on the last. Arrows, Home, End and typing a letter move; Esc and Tab close it. It flips at the viewport edge. The avatar is a UserMenu: the signed-in account heads its menu.
