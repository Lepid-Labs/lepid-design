lepid-design · direct manipulation

Range sliders

Native range inputs: the whole row takes a press, arrows step, Page Up/Down jump, Home and End go to the ends. A finger can't tell 47 from 48, so the box beside it takes an exact value and snaps it to a step.

Price

The thumbs stay $50 apart and never cross.

Date pickers

The native input brings the calendar, the keyboard, the locale and the phone's own wheel. Presets come first: most dates people pick are one of a few. A birthday is faster typed than paged to.

Today or within the next 90 days.

Typed: the digits fill in around the slashes.

Colour picker

Presets as a radio group (arrow keys move between them), the system picker for anything else, a hex field that takes #abc or 3366FF, and the colour on the real surface with its contrast ratio.

File upload

The dropzone is the file input's label: click, Enter or Space opens the picker. Dragging a file over changes the edge, the glow and the words before you let go. Each file has its own progress; a failure keeps the file for Retry.

    Drag and drop

    Drag a grip, or focus it and press Space: arrows move the item (Left and Right change column), Space drops, Escape puts it back. Each card's checkbox travels with it.

    Every step is announced: “Moved Task A to position 3 of 5 in Done.”

    Swipe actions

    On a touch screen (or with a mouse), swipe a row right to mark it read, left for Archive and Delete; pull all the way left to delete at once, with Undo. Vertical scrolling still works. The ⋯ menu has the same actions for everyone else.
