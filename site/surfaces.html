Inline editing

Click, press Enter or F2 to edit. Enter or clicking away saves, Escape puts the old text back, and focus returns to the text. The new value shows at once; if the save fails, the old value comes back and the input reopens with your draft. Touch screens always show the pencil.

Star rating

As an input it is a radio group: Tab reaches it once, the arrow keys choose, and hovering previews without committing. Read-only ratings fill to the exact average and print it with the count.

Live cursors and presence

The app reports positions ten times a second; each cursor glides between reports (it jumps under reduced motion). Colours come from a hash of the user id, so people keep theirs. A cursor that sits still fades out, and your own pointer is never drawn. The avatar stack says who is here.
