App shell

Optional header, optional left-hand nav, optional footer — leave a part out and its track collapses.

Parts

Narrow the window below 48rem: the nav becomes a drawer opened from the header toggle, or a bottom tab bar. Without a header, use the tab bar (AppShell picks it by default) — the drawer would have no toggle.

Header

Sticky, --ld-shell-header-h tall. Toggle, brand, .ld-topnav, then .ld-shell__actions pushed right.

Side nav

Sticky under the header; sections of icon + label items, a footer pinned low. Current page is aria-current="page".

Background

The shell paints the theme's .ld-bg treatment itself — no class on <body> needed.
