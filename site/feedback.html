Toasts

At most three show; the rest wait. Hover or focus the stack, or switch tabs, and every clock stops. Errors stay until dismissed and are read assertively. F8 jumps to the newest toast; Esc dismisses the one you are in.

Undo instead of confirm

Archiving happens at once; the commit waits out the toast's countdown, which pauses with it. Undo puts the row back; closing the toast (or leaving the page) commits.

    Autosave

    Saves 800 ms after you stop typing (5 s at most while you keep going). Only going offline, a failure and the recovery are announced. Toggle the network or make the next save fail.
